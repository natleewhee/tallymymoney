FINDING — foreign-currency purchases may only alert in the original
currency, not SGD:

PRD.md's non-goal says "foreign-currency transactions record the SGD
posted amount." This alert doesn't have one. Card networks post the FX
conversion to SGD days after the original charge, once cross-border
settlement completes — the real-time alert fires before that number
exists, so it cannot possibly be in this email. This isn't a Citi-specific
parsing gap; it's a timing fact about how card FX settlement works, and
will presumably recur for any bank's foreign-currency alert.

**This is not decided here.** Two real options for Nat to choose from,
not chosen in this document:
  (a) record the transaction in its original currency (JPY here), tag it
      immediately, and accept the SGD total is an approximation until/
      unless a later statement corrects it; or
  (b) hold foreign-currency transactions in a separate pending state and
      only surface them once a follow-up email (if the bank sends one)
      confirms the SGD amount — unknown yet whether Citi (or DBS/UOB/
      Trust) sends that follow-up at all.
