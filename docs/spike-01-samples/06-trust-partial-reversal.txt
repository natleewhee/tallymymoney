The schema in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md has no concept of "this transaction
reverses/adjusts that one." FR-17 (income matching, deferred to Phase 5)
was scoped around a different problem — a *payment* settling a *prior
expense* — not a same-day partial reversal from the *same bank* referring
to the *same purchase*. This is a smaller, more tractable version: same
merchant + same timestamp + opposite direction, arriving within minutes,
is a strong enough signal to auto-link without a manual matching UI.
Worth a small addition to Phase 3 (flag as a possible reversal, don't
auto-net) rather than waiting for the full Phase 5 income-matching design.
