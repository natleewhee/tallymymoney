Note: a fourth distinct UOB template ("A transaction of {amount} was
debited from your a/c {acct} at {time} {date}, SGT. Ref: {ref}."),
subject "UOB Transaction Alert" (no dash, differs from the card
templates' "UOB - Transaction Alert" and "UOB Transaction Alert" seen
elsewhere — (sender, subject) alone still isn't a reliable enough
fingerprint to skip body parsing, confirming the same lesson from
07-dbs-paynow-received.txt). Also a different date/time format from
every other UOB parser here (see parseUobGiroDate in dates.ts): time
and date are separate tokens ("06:54PM" / "7-Sep-2026"), day-Mon-year
with single dashes. Only the "debited" direction is confirmed; a
symmetric "credited" GIRO alert isn't handled since no such sample has
been seen yet.
